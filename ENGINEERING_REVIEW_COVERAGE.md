## Scope of review

The review covered repository structure, notebooks and Python code, portability, dependency declarations, stale outputs, static validation, tests where appropriate, data leakage risks, split strategy, artifact handling, and documentation completeness.

## Honest limitation

Most projects do not include their original datasets or heavy runtime artifacts. Therefore the reports distinguish static/code-level validation from end-to-end training, inference, dashboard, or data-pipeline execution. No metrics were invented. Projects must be rerun with their authorized data and locked environments before production claims.
